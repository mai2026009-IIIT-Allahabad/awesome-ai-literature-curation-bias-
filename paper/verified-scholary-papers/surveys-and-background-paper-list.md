# Surveys & Background

Broader reviews and conceptual papers that help readers understand
bias in AI and large language models.

---

## Bias in Large Language Models: Origin, Evaluation, and Mitigation

**Authors:** Yufei Guo et al.

**Year:** 2024

**Why it matters:**  
Provides a broad overview of where LLM bias comes from, how it can
be evaluated, and how it can be mitigated.

**Paper:** [Read the paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10915)

## Bias Is a Math Problem, AI Bias Is a Technical Problem: 10-Year Literature Review of AI/LLM Bias Research

**Authors:** Sourojit Ghosh, Kyra Wilson

**Year:** 2025

**Why it matters:**  
Reviews a decade of AI/LLM bias research and highlights gaps in how
bias is studied across the field.

**Paper:** [Read the paper](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AIES/article/view/36613)
