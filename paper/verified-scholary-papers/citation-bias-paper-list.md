# Citation Bias

Papers studying how citation counts, citation patterns, and
LLM-based recommendations can reproduce or amplify existing
citation inequalities.

---

## Who Gets Recommended? Investigating Gender, Race, and Country Disparities in Paper Recommendations from Large Language Models

**Authors:** Yifan Tian, Yixin Liu, Yi Bu, Jiqun Liu

**Year:** 2025

**Why it matters:**  
Directly investigates LLM-based literature recommendations and finds
that recommended papers tend to have higher citation counts, later
publication dates, and larger author teams.

**Paper:** [Read the paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00367)

---

## Large Language Models Reflect Human Citation Patterns with a Heightened Citation Bias

**Authors:** Andres Algaba et al.

**Year:** 2024

**Why it matters:**  
Shows how LLM-generated citations can reproduce human citation patterns
while exhibiting heightened citation bias.

**Paper:** [Read the paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15739)

---

## Citation Accuracy, Citation Noise, and Citation Bias: A Foundation of Citation Analysis

**Authors:** Lutz Bornmann, Christian Leibel

**Year:** 2025

**Why it matters:**  
Explains why citation counts can contain noise and bias and therefore
should not automatically be treated as measures of research quality.

**Paper:** [Read the paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12735)

---

## Viewing Citation Analysis Through the Lens of Citation Justice

**Authors:** Linda C. Smith

**Year:** 2026

**Why it matters:**  
Introduces the idea of citation justice and questions whether
citation-based evaluation fairly represents scholarly contributions.

**Paper:** [Read the paper](https://kula.uvic.ca/index.php/kula/article/view/305)

---

## Matthew Effects in Science and the Serial Diffusion of Ideas

**Authors:** Rudolf Farys, Tobias Wolbring

**Year:** 2021

**Why it matters:**  
Provides evidence for the Matthew effect, where already-recognized
research can accumulate additional citation advantages.

**Paper:** [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1162/qss_a_00129)
