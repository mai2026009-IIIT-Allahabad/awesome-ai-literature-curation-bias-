# Language Bias

Papers exploring how English-language dominance, linguistic differences,
and exclusion of non-English research can affect scientific knowledge
and AI systems.

---

## Exploratory Evaluation of Large Language Models for Reducing Language Bias in Systematic Review Screening

**Authors:** Junki Ikeguchi, Hiroaki Ueshima, Hiroshi Tamura

**Year:** 2026

**Why it matters:**  
Evaluates whether multilingual LLMs can reduce language-related bias
when screening research for systematic reviews.

**Paper:** [Read the paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42393975/)

---

## Linguistic Bias in ChatGPT: Language Models Reinforce Dialect Discrimination

**Authors:** Eve Fleisig et al.

**Year:** 2024

**Why it matters:**  
Shows that ChatGPT can favor standard English varieties and produce
more problematic responses toward non-standard dialects.

**Paper:** [Read the paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.750/)

---

## Addressing Linguistic Bias through a Contrastive Analysis of Academic Writing in the NLP Domain

**Authors:** Robert Ridley et al.

**Year:** 2023

**Why it matters:**  
Studies linguistic differences in NLP academic writing and their
relationship to linguistic bias in scientific publishing.

**Paper:** [Read the paper](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.1042/)

---

## Language Bias in Systematic Reviews: You Only Get Out What You Put In

**Authors:** C. Stern, J. Kleijnen

**Year:** 2020

**Why it matters:**  
Shows how language restrictions during literature searching can affect
which evidence is included in systematic reviews.

**Paper:** [Read the paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32925418/)

---

## The Prevalence of and Factors Associated with Inclusion of Non-English Language Studies in Campbell Systematic Reviews

**Authors:** Lauge Neimann Rasmussen, Paul Montgomery

**Year:** 2018

**Why it matters:**  
Provides empirical evidence that non-English studies are often
underrepresented in systematic reviews.

**Paper:** [Read the paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6107944/)

---

## Cultural Bias and Linguistic Representation in AI Curriculum Design

**Authors:** Nurhayati Usman et al.

**Year:** 2025

**Why it matters:**  
Discusses cultural and linguistic representation in AI education,
including challenges faced by low-resource languages.

**Paper:** [Read the paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/394011793_Cultural_Bias_and_Linguistic_Representation_in_AI_Curriculum_Design_Implications_for_Society_50)
