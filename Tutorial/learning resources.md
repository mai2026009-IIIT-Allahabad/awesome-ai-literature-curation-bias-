# 📚 Learning Resources

New to citation bias, bibliometrics, or AI literature curation?

The resources below provide a starting point for understanding the
concepts behind this repository.

---

## 1. Introduction to Bibliometrics

**NIH Library**

A beginner-friendly introduction to bibliometrics, citation analysis,
research metrics, and their limitations.

🔗 [Learn more](https://www.nihlibrary.nih.gov/training-category/bibliometrics-classes)

---

## 2. Leiden Manifesto for Research Metrics

**Leiden Manifesto**

Introduces 10 principles for responsible use of research metrics and
explains why quantitative indicators should not replace expert judgment.

🔗 [Learn more](https://www.leidenmanifesto.org/)

---

## 3. Cochrane Handbook – Searching and Selecting Studies

**Cochrane**

Learn about systematic literature searching, study selection, search
strategies, and the potential for language and publication bias.

🔗 [Learn more](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-04)

---

## 4. Responsible Use of Quantitative Indicators

**DORA**

A practical guide to understanding citation counts, h-index,
journal metrics, and other research indicators responsibly.

🔗 [Learn more](https://sfdora.org/resource/guidance-on-the-responsible-use-of-quantitative-indicators-in-research-assessment/)

---

## 5. OpenAlex

**OpenAlex**

An open scholarly-data platform that can be used to explore papers,
authors, institutions, topics, and citation relationships.

🔗 [Learn more](https://help.openalex.org/api/)

---

## 🧭 Suggested Learning Path

If you are completely new to the topic, start in this order:

**Bibliometrics**
↓  
**Citation Metrics**
↓  
**Research Evaluation**
↓  
**Language Bias in Literature Searching**
↓  
**Scholarly Data & AI Literature Curation**
